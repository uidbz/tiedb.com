I'm Johan Straarup. I build local-first tools in Go: a library for my files,
music and photos; the native apps I want to use it with; and a coding agent that
can learn from its own work.

The projects started in 2023 as **tiedb**, a key-value-value store. The file
tagger and the content-addressed filehost came later, and the tag tree made the
ideas fit together. The library is now `tie`, the apps are in `tie-gui`, and the
agent is `anneal`. They share a theme, a URL scheme and a conviction that the
data should stay on the machine that made it.

## What I care about

- **Local-first.** The default is no server, no account and no telemetry. The
  library is a file; the services are optional.
- **Native.** A Go binary that starts in a frame, not a browser tab. Light and
  dark themes are designed, not inverted.
- **Evidence.** The numbers on this site link to the engineering note they came
  from. Reverted experiments are written down. Limits are on the page.
- **Small pieces.** The gallery engine, the theme, the audio engine and the
  scripting layer are libraries, not a monolith.
- **No lock-in.** Content hashes, TSV dumps and a single-file store. If you want
  to leave, you can.

## AI assistance

Much of the code and prose is written with AI assistance, reviewed and tested by

process and the numbers are the parts I would want a reader to check first.
