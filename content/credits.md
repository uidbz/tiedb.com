## Software

- **[Hugo](https://gohugo.io)** — the static site generator. Apache-2.0.
- **[Hextra](https://github.com/imfing/hextra)** — the theme. MIT.
- **[Fyne](https://fyne.io)** — the GUI toolkit behind every app. BSD-3.
- **[Risor](https://risor.io)** and **[fynerisor](https://github.com/uidbz/fynerisor)** — the scripting layer. BSD-3.
- **[libmpv](https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv)** — video playback and frame extraction. GPL/LGPL.
- **[PipeWire](https://pipewire.org)** — audio. MIT.
- **Noto Sans** — the system font stack in the site and the apps. SIL OFL 1.1.

## Icons

The file-type icons in tie-fm are from KDE's **Breeze** icon theme
([LGPL-3+](https://invent.kde.org/frameworks/breeze-icons)). Everything else is
drawn for this site or comes from Fyne's built-in icon set.

## The logo family

The marks are generated by `tools/` from `tools/specs/marks.yaml`. anneal's
original geometry is preserved; the heavy stroke that gives the family its look
is the same one Fyne's SVG renderer produces at 26 px. The story is in
[The logo that was a bug](/blog/the-logo-that-was-a-bug/).
