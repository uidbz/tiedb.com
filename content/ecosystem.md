The projects share more than a colour palette. Each library exists because one of
the products needed it, and each one is usable on its own.

## The website itself

This site is Hugo and Hextra, with no JavaScript framework, no web fonts and no
CDNs. The logos are generated by `make logos`, the screenshots by `make shots`,
and the version facts by `make facts`. The source is in
[uidbz/tiedb.com](https://github.com/uidbz/tiedb.com).
